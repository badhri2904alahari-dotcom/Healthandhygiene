Health & Hygiene Fundamentals

Healthy Habits, Clean Living, and a Better You

1. What is Hygiene?

Hygiene refers to essential daily practices that maintain wellness and halt the spread of diseases—such as thorough hand washing, oral care, and clean living spaces.

2. What is the Difference?

Health represents a complete state of physical, mental, and social well-being.

Hygiene represents the practical steps and habits that help build and preserve that healthy state.

3. Why is Personal Hygiene Important?

It shields against infectious illnesses, elevates self-confidence, boosts mental clarity, and promotes vibrant long-term wellness.

4. Key Daily Examples

  • Bathing regularly with clean water
  • Washing hands thoroughly with soap
  • Brushing & flossing teeth twice daily
  • Washing hands before & after meals
  • Trimming and cleaning nails
  • Wearing fresh, clean clothing daily
